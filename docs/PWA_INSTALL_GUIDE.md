# Installing the Growize App

Welcome to Growize. You can add the app to your phone or computer in under a
minute — straight from your web browser, with no app store needed. Once it's
added, you'll have a Growize icon you can tap like any other app, and it opens
full-screen.

**Open this address in your browser to begin:**

## 👉 https://growizefarm.com

You only need to do this once.

---

## On an Android phone

1. Open **Chrome** and go to **growizefarm.com**.
2. Tap the menu (the **⋮** in the top-right corner).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (it may say **Install app**).
4. Tap **Add** to confirm.

The Growize icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it any time to open the app.

---

## On an iPhone or iPad

Please use the **Safari** browser for this — it's the only one Apple allows for
adding apps.

1. Open **Safari** and go to **growizefarm.com**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

The Growize icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it any time to open the app.

---

## On a computer (Windows or Mac)

1. Open **Chrome** or **Edge** and go to **growizefarm.com**.
2. Look for a small **install icon** at the right-hand end of the address bar
   and click it. (You can also open the **⋮** menu and choose **Install
   Growize**.)
3. Click **Install**.

Growize will open in its own window and be added to your apps so you can launch
it whenever you like.

---

## Signing in

1. Open the app and enter the **email address** you registered with ARL.
2. Tap **Send Code**. We'll email you a **6-digit code**.
3. Enter the code to sign in. There's no password to remember.
4. On a phone, you can set up a fingerprint, face unlock, or a short PIN so you
   can get back in quickly and securely next time.

Didn't get the code? Check your spam or junk folder, make sure you used the email
ARL has on file, and tap **Resend** after a few seconds.

---

## A few good things to know

- **It stays up to date on its own.** When we release improvements, you'll
  simply get the latest version next time you open the app — nothing to reinstall.
- **Your information is private and secure.** You only ever see your own
  portfolio, and your sign-in is protected by a one-time code plus the unlock you
  set on your device.
- **You can remove it any time.** Just press and hold the icon and choose to
  remove it (on a computer, uninstall it from your apps). Your account stays
  safe — you can always reopen growizefarm.com and add it again.
