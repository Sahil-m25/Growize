Privacy Policy

Agri Research Labs Pvt. Ltd. ("ARL")  ·  Effective: June 2026  ·  Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Agri Research Labs Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "ARL") handles information in connection with the Growize investor portal ("Platform"). The Platform is invite-only and accessible exclusively to investors who have entered into a formal investment relationship with ARL.

1. Who This Applies To

The Platform is not open to the general public. Access is limited to individuals who have been personally onboarded by ARL as investors in one of its agricultural LLP projects. We do not collect information from the public, and no user can self-register.

2. Information We Display (Not Collect)

We display information that already exists as part of your investment relationship with ARL, including:

  • Your name and contact details (provided during KYC at the time of investment)
  • Your investment portfolio details (units held, capital invested, payouts received)
  • Project updates and documents related to your investment

We do not independently collect personal data. All information shown on the Platform was provided by you or your authorised representative during the offline onboarding process.

3. Authentication

We use one-time password (OTP) authentication via email to verify your identity before granting access to your portfolio. We do not store passwords. OTPs expire within minutes and are single-use.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your portfolio data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase, hosted on AWS in the Asia-Pacific region). Access is restricted by row-level security policies ensuring you can only view your own data. We use TLS encryption for all data in transit.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is only shared:

  • With our technology service providers (cloud infrastructure, crash-reporting) strictly for operating the Platform
  • As required by applicable Indian law or a valid court order

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The Platform stores minimal data locally on your device (browser session state and cached portfolio data for offline viewing). This data is encrypted and never transmitted to third parties.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at tech@agresearchlabs.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your investment with ARL is active, and for a period of seven years thereafter as required by Indian financial regulations.

9. Contact

For privacy-related queries: tech@agresearchlabs.com
