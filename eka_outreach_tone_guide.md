# EKA Outreach — Tone & Template Reference

Source: `eka_9tp_upgraded_outreach_system.html` (Project EKA upgraded 9-touchpoint system)
Last updated: 04 May 2026

This document extracts the tone rules, template structures, and voice guidelines from the EKA outreach system for use by the daily email drafting automation.

---

## Core Tone Principles

1. **Lead with their pain, not your company name.** Open with what matters to the prospect's clients, not with a self-introduction.
2. **Credibility first, numbers never in cold outreach.** Mention IIMB, Blinkit, BigBasket, Samunnati, EBITDA positive. Save yield percentages for warm/founder phase.
3. **One CTA per email.** Either a Calendly link OR "reply and I'll send the brief." Never both.
4. **Short.** LinkedIn DMs: max 3 lines. Emails: max 6-8 short paragraphs. No walls of text.
5. **End with a question or a specific ask.** Not "let me know your thoughts."
6. **Subject lines do 80% of the work.** A/B test. Use peer names, specific claims, or pattern-interrupt phrasing.
7. **P.S. lines are mandatory on Email 1.** Use for social proof (peer names) or urgency (Phase 1 close date).
8. **No AI language.** Zero tolerance for: "I wanted to reach out", "I hope this finds you well", "leveraging", "synergies", "exploring potential opportunities", "in this space", "aligns well with", "I came across your profile", "I'd love to connect."

## Voice by Track

### Track 2 — Cold Outreach (Jhalak sends)

**Persona:** Sharp, professional BD executive. Gets to the point. Warm but efficient. No fluff.

**LinkedIn DM (D3) rules:**
- Max 3 lines
- Lead with their client's world, not ARL
- End with a question: "Worth 15 minutes?"
- If they posted recently, open with it: "Saw your note on [topic] last week — relevant timing."

**Email 1 (D5) rules:**
- Subject line options (A/B test per batch):
  - Peer reference: "Sridhar at B32 is already in the founding cohort"
  - Credibility hook: "Blinkit's farm supplier — now open to WM distribution"
  - Curiosity: "A real-asset product your HNI clients probably haven't seen"
- Body structure: 1 paragraph credibility (IIMB, Blinkit, BigBasket, Samunnati, EBITDA positive) + 1 paragraph product (asset-backed LLP, ARL-operated, tax-free ag income Month 7, capital returned Year 5) + CTA (Calendly link)
- Mandatory P.S. with social proof
- Sign-off: "Pradeep Ram, AgResearch Labs" (even cold emails come from founder name)

**Email 2 (D8) rules:**
- Reply to previous subject ("Re: [previous subject]")
- One new fact (e.g., Phase 1 unit count, units confirmed)
- One pre-empted objection (rotate by prospect tier: WMs = capital protection, direct investors = liquidity, NRIs = repatriation)
- Short. 3-4 paragraphs max.
- P.S. for CPs: commission teaser

**Final Nudge (D18) rules:**
- Real date, real consequence ("Phase 1 closes 31 May")
- Referral ask: "If you know one advisor whose clients look at real-asset alternatives, I'd appreciate a name."
- Phase 2 door open: "I'll send you a note when Phase 2 opens in July."
- Clean close — never desperate

### Track 3 — Warm Referral (Jhalak sends, mentions Pradeep)

**Persona:** Warmer than cold. Jhalak is connecting them because Pradeep recommended it. Tone shifts from "stranger pitch" to "warm introduction."

**Warm Email 1 (W1):**
- Open with the referral: "Pradeep suggested I reach out..." or "Pradeep mentioned your name in the context of..."
- Brief credibility (1 line)
- Product summary (2 lines)
- CTA: Calendly or "reply and I'll send the brief"
- Sign-off: "Warm regards, Jhalak"

**Warm Follow-up (W2):**
- Shorter. Reference previous email.
- Add one new angle (site visit offer, peer reference, new data point)

**Warm WA/Phone (W3):**
- WhatsApp preferred. Voice note if comfortable (25-35 seconds).
- No links on first WhatsApp. Curiosity only.
- Text fallback: 3 lines max, end with "Worth a 15-min call this week?"

### Track 4 — Founder Close (Pradeep sends personally)

**Persona:** Confident founder energy. Speaks like someone who has built something real and wants to share, not sell. Direct. No hedging. No corporate speak. Peer-to-peer.

**Founder Email 1 (F1):**
- Subject: "Quick thought / [Company Name]"
- Opens peer-to-peer: reference their specific firm and role
- Growize value prop in their language (not ARL's marketing copy)
- CTA: 20-minute call
- Sign-off: "Best, Pradeep"

**Founder Follow-up (F2):**
- Reply to previous subject
- One new credibility point or social proof
- Shorter than F1

**Founder Final Ask (F3):**
- Real close date. Real consequence.
- Referral ask (same as cold breakup).
- Phase 2 door open.
- Clean, dignified close.

## Prospect-Tier Personalization

Rotate messaging angle based on prospect type:

| Tier | Pain point to lead with | Objection to pre-empt |
|------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Wealth Manager | "Your clients earn 3-4% residential yield" | Capital protection |
| Family Office | "One gap: real-asset product with operating income, not just appreciation" | Structure complexity |
| NRI Advisor | "India exposure without India presence + tax-free ag income under Section 10(1)" | Repatriation / legal |
| RE Advisor | "3-5% residential isn't cutting it — direct conceptual substitute" | Liquidity / exit |

## Objection Handling (for reply drafts)

When a prospect replies with an objection, draft the response using these frameworks:

**"What protects my capital?"** — You own land, not a promise. LLP holds registered title deed. Milestone-linked disbursement. Offer to share legal structure in 15 minutes.

**"Is this SEBI regulated?"** — LLP structure governed by LLP Act 2008, not SEBI. ARL is operator/EPC contractor, not investment advisor. Legal opinion available.

**"Explain the LLP simply"** — "Think of it like a housing society — but instead of a flat, you own a share of an agricultural entity that holds land." One unit = 25L.

**"What's the exit before Year 5?"** — Be honest: 5-year structure, not liquid. Two mechanisms: secondary transfer, case-by-case discussion. Qualifying question — wrong-fit investors should be told upfront.

**"Will the farm produce?"** — 12 years operating. 7x yields, 40% lower cost. Lease rental not tied to crop fluctuations. Site visit offer.

## Post-Call Path (for Deal Stage progression)

When AH = "Call Done", draft post-call emails using:

**PC1 (same day, within 3 hours):** Recap + deck + 3 attachments (brief, terms, LLP overview) + Calendly for second call. P.S. with peer reference offer.

**PC2 (Day 3):** Dataroom link + "3 questions your CA will probably ask" (pre-answered).

**PC3 (Day 7):** Token commitment ask. 2.5L advance (10%) holds allocation. Direct ask, not hint.

**PC4 (Day 14):** Allocation expiry. Real date, real consequence. Phase 2 opt-in alternative.

## Humanizer Checklist (mandatory before sending any draft)

Every drafted email MUST pass through these checks:

1. **Delete AI phrases:** "I wanted to reach out", "I hope this finds you well", "leveraging", "synergies", "exploring potential opportunities", "in this space", "on my radar", "aligns well with", "I came across your profile", "I'd love to connect", "streamline", "cutting-edge", "game-changing", "innovative solution"
2. **Use contractions:** "we've" not "we have", "I'd" not "I would", "there's" not "there is", "don't" not "do not"
3. **Vary sentence length:** Mix 5-word punches with 25-word explanations. Never uniform.
4. **Add natural imperfections:** Dash instead of comma occasionally. Sentence fragment for emphasis. Start one sentence with "And" or "But".
5. **Read aloud test:** If any phrase sounds like a template or AI wrote it, rewrite it.
6. **Check subject line:** Does it pass the "would I open this?" test? Peer names > clever wordplay > generic intros.
7. **One CTA only:** Never two asks in one email.
8. **P.S. present on Email 1:** Social proof or urgency.
